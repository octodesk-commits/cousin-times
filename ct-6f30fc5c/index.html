Kilkee and Loop Head by Car: The West Clare Coast

The best cliffs in County Clare have no visitor centre, no coach park, no audio guide and no ticket gate. They have a small car park, a footpath that ends at an unfenced edge, and on a clear day a view north to the Twelve Pins and south to the Blaskets. That is Loop Head, and most people who come to Clare for the Cliffs of Moher never see it. Kilkee is where the drive starts — a Victorian bathing town wrapped round a horseshoe bay and the last place on the peninsula with fuel, a supermarket and a real choice of places to eat. After that the road narrows and the county runs out into the Atlantic, so the trade-off is simple: scenery without infrastructure, and a day that needs planning.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
