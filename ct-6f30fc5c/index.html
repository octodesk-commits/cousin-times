Cahir Castle by Car: The River Suir, the Swiss Cottage and Where to Park

Cahir is the rare Irish castle you can park beside and walk into in the clothes you drove there in. It stands on a rocky island in the River Suir at the bottom of the town's main street, the car park is at the foot of the walls, and there is no hill, no shuttle and no coach queue to work around. That ease is the problem. For years it was the quick stop between Cashel and Cork — forty minutes round the walls, a photograph of the gatehouse, back in the car — and that is the wrong way to do it. The castle is worth the hour, but the reason to stop here rather than at any other castle on this road is the two flat kilometres of riverside path behind the town and the small thatched cottage at the end of it. Here is what it costs, where the car goes, and the one thing to check before planning the walk.
