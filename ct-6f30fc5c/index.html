From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Killarney at Christmas: The Lights, the Markets and the Park

Killarney does not do a Christmas market the way Waterford or Belfast does. It does a Christmas season — six weeks, mid-November to the end of December, laid over a town you can walk across in ten minutes and a national park that begins at the bottom of the main street. Lights go up along the approach roads, toy-soldier bollards stand along the footpaths, and a postbox for Santa appears in the centre. One complication first: a good share of the people searching for Christmas in Killarney want the song, not the town — written in 1950, recorded by Bing Crosby in October 1951. It is lovely, it is on every streaming service, and it is not a travel guide. The practical half matters more: what is on, what it costs, and where the car goes on the four evenings the centre closes to traffic.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
