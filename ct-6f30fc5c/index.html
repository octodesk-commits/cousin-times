From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Christmas in Dublin: Lights, Markets and Where to Park

Grafton Street switches on in the middle of November, and for about seven weeks Dublin stops pretending to be a normal working city. Chandeliers go up on wires above the full length of the street, the shops stay open late and the whole centre glows. The part that catches visitors out is the clock — in December, Dublin is dark by five. If you are hiring a car, the first decision is which half of the trip it is for, because the centre is small, congested, and parking is the thing cousins consistently get wrong. This is the honest version: where the lights are, what the markets actually are this season, where to leave the car, and the three things that are not worth the trouble.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
