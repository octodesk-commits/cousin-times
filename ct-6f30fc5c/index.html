From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

County Mayo and Connemara by Car: The Complete Self-Drive Guide

Most people who come to the west of Ireland decide in advance which county they belong to, and then plan a trip that proves it — Mayo or Galway, Clew Bay or Connemara, the mountain or the fjord. The mistake is in the question. They were never two trips: this is one continuous stretch of Atlantic coast, about two hours end to end, and the border between them is a line on a map that no road sign on the N59 makes any fuss about. This is the guide I would give a cousin landing at Knock or Shannon with a week and a car.
