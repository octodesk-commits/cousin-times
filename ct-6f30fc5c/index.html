The Rock of Cashel by Car: Parking, Tickets and the Walk Up

For a place that draws hundreds of thousands of visitors a year, the Rock of Cashel is oddly hard to arrive at. You cannot drive to it. The car park sits at the foot of the hill, about two hundred metres below the entrance, and everything above that point is on foot. The €4.50 you pay down there goes to Tipperary County Council, not to the site, and the ticket that gets you inside is bought at the top. Two payments, one hill, and opening hours that lose an hour of the afternoon in winter. Most people meet the Rock as a forty-five-minute stop between Dublin and Cork, which is why so many leave without seeing the best of it.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
