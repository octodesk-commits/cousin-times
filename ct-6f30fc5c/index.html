Kylemore Abbey by Car: Parking, Timing and the Victorian Walled Garden

The photograph everyone comes for is taken from a bridge on the N59, standing still while the traffic goes past behind you. Kylemore Abbey sits low against the water, a mountain comes down steeply behind it, and on a still morning the whole building is doubled in the lake — one of the most photographed views in Ireland, and it costs nothing at all. Then most people check the time, work out that the estate is a paid attraction with a car park and a shuttle bus, and drive on. That is the mistake: Kylemore is a thousand acres of mountain, lake, woodland and garden, and the walled garden at the far end is the best thing on it.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
