From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Cliffs of Moher by Car: Parking, Timing and the Visitor Centre

I once sat in a queue outside Liscannor for forty minutes on a Tuesday in July, watching a family from Ohio work out that the car park is on the opposite side of the road from the cliffs, and that you drive to an admission pod with a booking rather than a barrier with a ticket machine. They had been planning the drive for a year; nobody had mentioned the pod. Here is the counter-intuitive part: the cliffs cost nothing and always have. What you buy at the gate is a car park, safe paths and a two-hour slot — so the two things that decide whether you enjoy the Cliffs of Moher are a booking time and an arrival time, not the scenery. The scenery has never had an off day.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
