Knock Shrine and Ballintubber Abbey by Car: Mayo's Pilgrim Roads

The gable wall at Knock is still standing, and it is the whole reason a village of a few hundred people in east Mayo receives well over a million visitors a year — fifteen parishioners stood in pouring rain before that wall in August 1879 and watched a still, silent scene for close to two hours. Thirty-five minutes west is the other half of the story: Ballintubber Abbey, where Mass has been said without a break for more than eight hundred years, through the dissolution, a fire in the 1650s and the penal laws. One is free, open every day, and busier than you expect; the other is quieter than its history deserves.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
