From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Tipperary Town by Car: The Vee, the Glen and the Road to Cork

A cousin from Boston drove Dublin to Cork last September and texted from Tipperary at four in the afternoon to say the town had nothing in it. He had parked on Main Street, walked its length twice, photographed the Memorial Arch and got back in the car. He never turned left at the top of the street, which is where the only road here worth driving begins. That is the problem with Tipperary town: it is a base, not an attraction, and almost nobody treats it as either. It holds half an hour of real history in one street, it is the quietest place to sleep if you want the Galtee Mountains on your doorstep, and it sits on the N24 exactly where the drive to Cork turns south. The honest advice: if your itinerary is Cashel, Cahir and Cork, sleep in Cashel or Cahir. Come here for the mountains — the Glen of Aherlow is four miles from the square.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
