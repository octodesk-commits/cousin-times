Clonakilty and West Cork by Car: Beaches, Music and Black Pudding

I have had cousins fly into Cork Airport with a single instruction: just show us the real West Cork. They do not want the coach-tour version. They want the beach where locals walk the dog before work, the pub where the session starts because someone left a fiddle on a stool, and the small town that somehow produces Ireland's most famous black pudding. That town is Clonakilty.
