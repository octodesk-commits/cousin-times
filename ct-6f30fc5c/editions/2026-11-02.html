From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Bunratty Castle and Folk Park by Car

A cousin of mine landed at Shannon on the overnight from Boston, picked up a car at nine in the morning, and was standing under the battlements of a fifteenth-century tower house before half past ten. That is the whole appeal of Bunratty — ten minutes from the airport road, and a real medieval keep rather than a reproduction, Great Hall, chapel and battlements all up the same stone stairs. It is also the County Clare stop that goes wrong most often: the castle stops admitting people at four o'clock sharp, and a great many visitors reach the door at ten past, having treated Bunratty as a ninety-minute photo stop on the way somewhere else.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
