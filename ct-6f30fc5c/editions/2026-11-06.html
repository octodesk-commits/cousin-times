From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Lahinch by Car: The Promenade, the Surf and the Golf Links

Lahinch is one street, one promenade and close to two kilometres of sand, and the village is arranged entirely around the point where the three meet. You come off the N67 from Ennistymon past that tight bend, the road runs out, and there is the Atlantic directly in front of you with nowhere further to go — no bypass, no second entrance worth using. You drive in, you park, and for the rest of your stay you walk. That shape decides more of your day than you would expect: the tide sets what is on offer, where you leave the car settles whether you enjoy the place or spend twenty minutes circling it, and the two ends of the beach are two different afternoons.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
