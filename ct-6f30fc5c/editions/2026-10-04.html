Charles Fort Kinsale by Car: Parking and the Harbour View

The first time you stand on the ramparts of Charles Fort, the wind drops and the whole of Kinsale Harbour tilts into view. The town sits on the far shore, the boats are moored in neat rows, and the water changes colour with every shift of cloud. It is the kind of view that explains why somebody bothered to build a fortress here in the first place.
