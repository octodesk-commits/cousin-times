From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Dursey Island by Car: Cable Car, Loop Walk and the Atlantic End

I stood at the cable car station above Dursey Sound one morning in late April and watched the cabin come back empty. The wind was up, the sea below was the colour of slate, and a fisherman on the mainland side was coiling rope like he had all day. That is Dursey Island in a single image: a small red cable car crossing a narrow strip of Atlantic, and nobody rushing on either side.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
