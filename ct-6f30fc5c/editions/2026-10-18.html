From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Irish Etiquette and Customs First-Timers Should Know

The rules that catch visitors out in Ireland are almost never the written ones. A cousin of mine from Chicago spent her first evening in Westport buying a single drink for the man on the next stool — and quietly set four strangers into a round she never meant to start. Nobody corrected her. That is the Irish way: the social rules are real, they are enforced, and they are almost never explained to your face. The good news is that they are few and consistent, and once you know them you can stop thinking about them.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
