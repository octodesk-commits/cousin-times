From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Ireland Visa, Passport and Entry Requirements

I once had a cousin fly into Dublin with a passport that expired six weeks after her return flight. She had read somewhere that Ireland required six months' validity, and she had spent the previous evening in a panic, searching for an emergency appointment at the passport office. I told her to breathe. The Republic of Ireland does not enforce a six-month rule for US or Canadian visitors. Her passport only needed to be valid for the duration of her stay. She made it through immigration in ten minutes.
