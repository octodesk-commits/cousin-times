From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Ireland in November: What Is Open and What Is Not

November ends the Irish season without any ceremony at all. The Skellig Michael boats are tied up, the garden island at Glengarriff has shut its gates, the Cliffs of Moher open an hour later than they did in May and close four hours earlier, and the light is gone by half past four. None of it is announced — you simply arrive and find the gate locked. Most of what gets written about the month leads with the weather. The weather is poor, but you can waterproof a coat, and you cannot add an hour to a November afternoon. Daylight is the real constraint on what you can do, and the second is that a fair number of the small, out-of-the-way places worth driving to have closed until spring. The famous sites stay open, on shorter hours, in front of smaller crowds than at any other time of year.
