From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Ring of Beara by Car: The Full Loop, Healy Pass and Dursey

The first time you crest a bend on the Ring of Beara and the Atlantic falls away on your left with nothing but heather and rock between you and the water, you realise why the coach drivers stay on the other peninsula. The Ring of Kerry has the fame, the gift shops and the traffic jams at Ladies View. The Beara has the road to yourself, the sheep that own it, and the sense that you have driven past the end of the ordinary tourist map.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
