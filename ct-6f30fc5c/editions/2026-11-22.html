New Year's Eve in Dublin: Where to Be and How to Get Home

New Year's Eve in Dublin is not the night most visitors think they are booking. There is no public fireworks display in this season's programme, no countdown on O'Connell Street and no hilltop from which the city goes up at midnight. What Dublin does instead is build a three-day festival around one courtyard and leave the rest of the night to the pubs and the streets. That is not a criticism — it is the reason the night works, provided you plan it the way the city is actually shaped. It also means the hardest part of the evening is never the evening: it is the getting home. So this is the programme as it stands, where to stand at midnight with and without a ticket, what the trains and trams really do on a Thursday night in late December, and where the car should sleep. (First published here on 15 November; the Travel Desk has filed nothing for three mornings, so the desk re-runs the next date in the calendar.)
