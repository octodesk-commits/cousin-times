From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Leenane and Killary Harbour by Car: Ireland's Only Fjord

There is a stretch of the N59 just west of Leenane where the road stops being a road and becomes a shelf. On your right the mountain goes up; on your left it goes down, hard, into water the wrong colour for Ireland — black-green, deep, still. No beach, no shallow end: the land simply stops, and sixteen kilometres of drowned valley begins. Killary is the one place in the west where the mountains do not politely step back from the sea — they stand in it. (First published here on Monday; the Travel Desk has had nothing new since.)
