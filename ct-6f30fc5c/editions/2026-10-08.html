Tipping in Ireland: A Straight Guide for Visitors

You step out of a restaurant in Cork city at half ten, the bill still folded in your hand, and you realise you have no idea whether you were supposed to leave anything extra. The server was sound, the food was grand, but nobody on the staff said a word about tipping. That silence is normal here. Tipping in Ireland is not the performance it is in North America — it is optional, low-stakes, and mostly guided by rounding up rather than percentages.
