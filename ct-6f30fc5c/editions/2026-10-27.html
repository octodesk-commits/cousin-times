From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Cong and Ashford Castle by Car: The Quiet Man Country

The first thing you meet in Cong is a weathered limestone cross standing in the middle of the street with traffic going round it — said to mark the abbey's completion in the twelfth century. Further along, the abbey sits roofless beside the road, and the river that made the place runs under a low bridge at the bottom of the village. Cong is famous for one thing, a John Ford film shot here in 1951, and it is the least interesting thing about the place: there is also a canal that has never held water, a twenty-metre Victorian folly hidden in woods, and a castle you cannot get inside.

By Shruti, Travel Correspondent
