From the Travel Desk — My Irish Cousin

Money, Cards and Currency in Ireland

I watched a cousin from Boston try to pay for a coffee in Belfast with a twenty-euro note last summer. The barista was patient — Belfast gets plenty of confused visitors — but the queue behind her was not. That is the moment I realised most first-timers do not think about money as a planning item. They think about flights, hotels and the Cliffs of Moher. Then they land, order their first breakfast, and discover the practical stuff is where the friction lives.
